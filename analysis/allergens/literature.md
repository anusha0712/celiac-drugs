## 7. Figueiredo 2024 — excluded

Figueiredo et al. 2024 (Sci Rep 14:15631, PMID 38972872) was cited in the
compass-artifact primer and in the Phase 1 research brief `research_milk.md`
as a Portuguese study analyzing dairy-derived excipients. Per user direction
2026-04-21, this paper is not used as a source for the analysis and is not
included in the milk-literature table above. The paper remains in the
audit-layer research brief as a historical record of what the primer
surfaced.
