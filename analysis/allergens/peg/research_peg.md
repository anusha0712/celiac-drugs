# Polyethylene Glycol (PEG) as a Hidden Drug Allergen — Research File

Every factual claim below is anchored to a primary-source URL inline. UNII,
PMID, and CFR citations were independently verified against FDA GSRS,
PubMed, and Cornell on 2026-04-21. Claims the original primer made that
could not be verified have been removed.

**Scope note:** this project's `contains_peg` flag is restricted to
unconjugated PEG polymer (POLYETHYLENE GLYCOL, POLYETHYLENE OXIDE, MACROGOL,
CARBOWAX). Adjacent families (polysorbates, poloxamers, polyoxyl
derivatives, mPEG, PEG-N surfactants, TPGS, Kollicoat IR) are out of scope
for flagging and are discussed separately in `analysis/allergens/
methodology.md` and `analysis/allergens/limitations.md`. Background on
those families is retained here because they appear in the peer-reviewed
"PEG family" literature and the author may choose to discuss them.

## Harm anchor

- Stone CA Jr et al. "Immediate Hypersensitivity to Polyethylene Glycols
  and Polysorbates: More Common Than We Have Recognized." J Allergy Clin
  Immunol Pract 2019;7(5):1533-1540. Reports two index anaphylaxis cases
  (PEG 3350 bowel prep), both skin-test cross-reactive to polysorbate 80,
  plus 53 additional possible-PEG-3350-anaphylaxis FAERS cases.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30557713/
- Wolfson AR et al. "First-dose mRNA COVID-19 vaccine allergic reactions:
  limited role for excipient skin testing." J Allergy Clin Immunol Pract
  2021;9(9):3308-3320. MGH cohort of 80 patients skin-tested to PEG and
  polysorbate 80 after first-dose mRNA reactions.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34166844/
- Greenhawt M et al. "The risk of allergic reaction to SARS-CoV-2
  vaccines and recommended evaluation and management: A systematic
  review, meta-analysis, GRADE assessment, and international consensus
  approach." Allergy 2023;78(1):71-83. Reports PEG skin-test sensitivity
  0.02, specificity 0.99; polysorbate sensitivity 0.03, specificity 0.97.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36321821/

**Prevalence context:**
- Reker D et al. "'Inactive' ingredients in oral medications." Sci Transl
  Med 2019;11(483):eaau6753. Table 1 reports PEG in 36.03% of oral solid
  dosage formulations (Pillbox v201605; n=42,052). PEG and Poloxamer
  (0.76%) are listed as SEPARATE Table 1 categories. Polysorbates are NOT
  in Table 1. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30867323/ — PMC
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7122736/

**Excipient-allergy review framework:**
- Caballero ML et al. "Hidden dangers: recognizing excipients as potential
  causes of drug and vaccine hypersensitivity reactions." J Allergy Clin
  Immunol Pract 2021;9(8):2968-2982.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33737254/

## USP-NF PEG contaminant update (Sept 2023)

FDA/USP PEG monograph now requires DEG/EG contaminant testing for PEG
grades above MW 1000. This is a contaminant issue (relevant to the Oct
2022 Gambia/Indonesia cough-syrup deaths), not an allergen labeling issue.
https://www.uspnf.com/notices/peg-gen-announcement-20230929

## DailyMed bulk scan — PEG-related excipient strings

Scanned `data/fullcatalog/dailymed_fullcatalog_raw.csv` (199,961 rows; 10,019 unique excipient
strings). Case-insensitive regex search on PEG / macrogol / carbowax /
polyoxyl / poloxamer / polysorbate / pluronic / kolliphor / polyethylene
oxide / mPEG / tween / span / polyoxyethylene yielded 443 matched unique
strings.

**MW grades actually present in DailyMed under the unconjugated PEG family:**
PEG 200, 250, 300, 350, 400, 450, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900, 1000, 1100,
1450, 1500, 1600, 2000, 3000, 3350, 3500, 4000, 4500, 7000, 8000, 10000,
11000, 12000, 14000, 20000. Plus "POLYETHYLENE GLYCOL, UNSPECIFIED"
(12,154 rows), "POLYETHYLENE GLYCOL" (1,078 rows), "POLYETHYLENE GLYCOLS"
(1,197 rows). Polyethylene-oxide grades: PEO 100000, 200000, 300000,
600000, 900000, 1,000,000, 2,000,000, 5,000,000, 7,000,000.

**Propylene glycol** is present in 27,136 rows. Chemically distinct from
PEG (single 3-carbon diol, not a polymer); does NOT cross-react with PEG
in the IgE sense. Tracked separately if the project scopes in a fourth
allergen.

## Final DailyMed search term list (tight-scope PEG)

Regex patterns applied against uppercased `excipients_raw` tokens:

- `\bPOLYETHYLENE\s+GLYCOL` — catches all MW grades, unspecified, and
  plural
- `\bPOLYETHYLENE\s+OXIDE` — same polymer at higher MW
- `\bMACROGOL` — EU/USP-EP name; also catches `MACROGOLGLYCEROL`
- `\bCARBOWAX` — Dow trade name for PEG

Adjacent families (polysorbates, poloxamers, polyoxyl derivatives, mPEG,
PEG-N surfactants, TPGS, Kollicoat IR) are NOT in the `contains_peg`
flagger under the 2026-04-22 scope decision. See
`analysis/allergens/methodology.md` PEG section for the documented
rationale.
